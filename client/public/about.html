Play first. Decide second.

Discover indie games by playing them.

Scroll Games is an experimental, open-source discovery feed for short, touch-friendly game demos. Move between human-reviewed games, play immediately in your browser, then follow, wishlist, or download the ones that earn your attention.

Play the current catalog

Why this exists

Trailers show a game. A demo lets you feel it. Scroll Games removes the install step from discovery while giving independent developers a direct path to their store, wishlist, or community page.

What makes it different

  • Human-curated indie games instead of an unbounded AI-generated feed.
  • Playable demos before a download, purchase, or sign-up decision.
  • Developer-owned hosting and direct destination links.
  • An open catalog contract, public source code, and reviewable moderation rules.

Project status

Scroll Games is an early public prototype validating game supply, mobile loading speed, multi-game sessions, and downstream interest. Native app distribution and social features stay deferred until the web pilot proves demand.
