About GitHub

For indie developers

Let players feel your game before they install it.

Scroll Games is recruiting mobile-ready browser demos for its curated pilot. You keep ownership and hosting control. Players get a direct path to your wishlist, download, store, or community page.

Read the publishing guide

Pilot fit

  • A short HTML5 or WebGL demo that already works with touch controls.
  • A stable public HTTPS URL with fast mobile loading.
  • A destination where interested players can follow or get the full game.
  • Clear ownership, age suitability, and privacy disclosures.

What Scroll Games does not take

The pilot does not take ownership, require exclusivity, port native games to the web, or promise a specific audience size. Every game is reviewed before publication and can be hidden quickly if it becomes unsafe or unavailable.

Contribute to the platform

The player, catalog contract, validation scripts, and moderation documentation are public. Code, documentation, accessibility, testing, and design contributions are welcome.
