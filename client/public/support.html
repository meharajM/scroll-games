Player and developer help

Scroll Games support

If a trial does not load, first check your connection, reopen the app, and try the next game. WebGL trials may require a current device and browser.

Report a game

Open the in-app catalog, find the game, and choose Report. The private form returns a receipt when delivered and can immediately hide the game on your device. Reports can cover inappropriate content, an incorrect age rating, privacy concerns, impersonation, copyright, security, or a broken trial. Email remains available if private report delivery is temporarily unavailable.

Get help

Email meharaj137@gmail.com or search existing reports and open a support issue. Include the game ID, device model, OS version, and what happened. Never post credentials or other sensitive information.

Developer help

Game developers should follow the publishing guide and use the repository pull-request workflow.
