Privacy notice

Last updated: August 17, 2026

What the platform stores

Scroll Games stores your selected age group, hidden-game list, and a cached game catalog in your browser or app storage. This information stays on your device and is used only to filter and restore the feed.

Network and service data

The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to deliver and secure the service. Scroll Games does not require an account and does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or payment details.

Third-party game trials

Game trials are created and hosted by the developer named in each catalog entry. Loading a trial connects your device to that developer’s hosting service, which can receive standard network information. Trials run in a restricted frame without access to Scroll Games storage and are not granted camera, microphone, location, payment, or file permissions; the player does allow fullscreen and compatible game controllers. Developers must disclose any additional collection and comply with the platform publishing rules.

Developer destinations

Choosing a wishlist, download, buy, follow, or developer link opens an external website selected by the listed developer. The destination receives a normal web request and may receive the Scroll Games site origin as referral information. Its privacy terms apply after you leave Scroll Games.

Age filtering

Scroll Games is not directed to children under 13. The declared age group is a local content preference used to exclude trials marked for older audiences; it is not identity or age verification and is not sent to Scroll Games or game developers.

Choices and deletion

You can change your age group from the game catalog. Clearing the app or browser storage deletes the local preferences and saved catalog. You can hide an individual game from the catalog.

Game reports

If you submit an in-app report, Scroll Games sends the game ID, selected concern, your written details, a random receipt ID, and timestamp to the maintainer’s private moderation destination. A one-way network-address hash and recent timestamps can remain in bounded, ephemeral server memory to limit abuse; the raw address and hash are not included in the report payload. Do not put personal information in report details.

Questions or requests

Email meharaj137@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. You can also use the project issue tracker, but never include private or sensitive information in a public issue.
