# Scroll Games brand and writing guide

## Positioning

**Primary tagline:** Play indie games before you download.

**One sentence:** Scroll Games is a human-curated, open-source feed of short indie game demos that players can try instantly before they wishlist, download, buy, or follow the full game.

**Player promise:** Feel the game before committing time, storage, or money.

**Developer promise:** Keep ownership and hosting control while sending qualified players to a destination you choose.

## Differentiation

Lead with:

1. Human-curated, real indie games.
2. Play before installation.
3. A direct path to the developer’s destination.
4. Open-source rules and infrastructure.

Do not position Scroll Games as “TikTok for games,” an AI game generator, a general game portal, an app store replacement, or an open upload service.

## Voice

The voice is direct, curious, and editorial. It respects players’ time and developers’ ownership.

- Prefer “Play the demo” over “Experience content.”
- Prefer “Get the game” over “Proceed to external destination.”
- Prefer “Human-curated” over “handpicked by our revolutionary platform.”
- Describe prototype limits plainly. Never invent catalog size, audience, conversion, privacy, personalization, or monetization claims.

## Search descriptions

Use naturally where relevant; do not repeat them as keyword lists:

- play indie games online
- indie game demos
- browser game trials
- discover indie games
- mobile HTML5 games
- open-source game discovery

Approved homepage description:

> Play human-curated indie game demos instantly, then follow, wishlist, or download the games you love. Open source and built for mobile.

Approved developer-page description:

> Submit a touch-friendly HTML5 or WebGL indie game demo to the open-source Scroll Games discovery feed.

## Visual system

The direction is **editorial arcade**: warm paper, dark ink, and a single signal-orange accent. Flat color and strong type replace neon gradients, glass effects, and generic gaming purple.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#fff1d6` | Light backgrounds and text on dark surfaces. |
| Paper muted | `#ead7b7` | Secondary text on dark surfaces. |
| Ink | `#1b1712` | Primary text and dark backgrounds. |
| Surface | `#28221b` | Raised dark UI surfaces. |
| Signal orange | `#f05a28` | Brand mark, highlights, and active controls. |
| Dark orange | `#a63b18` | Accessible links and primary destination actions. |

Typography uses `Avenir Next` when available, followed by Avenir, Trebuchet MS, and a rounded system sans-serif. Avoid adding a remote font until measured brand value outweighs privacy, loading, and layout-shift costs.

## Assets

- Primary mark: `client/public/icons/scroll-games.svg`
- Web icons: `client/public/icons/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png`
- Social card: `client/public/og-card.png`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width. Do not recolor individual path elements, add gradients, stretch the mark, or place it on a low-contrast background.
