# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Scroll Games contributors, maintainers, developers, and players deserve a welcoming environment free from harassment. We commit to making participation respectful regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.
- Keep private information, credentials, and security details out of public discussion.
- Respect game creators’ rights and do not submit content you do not own or have permission to distribute.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Impersonation, deceptive submissions, malware, rights infringement, or attempts to bypass review controls.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report community-conduct concerns privately to `meharaj137@gmail.com`. Include relevant links or context, but do not send unnecessary personal information. Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical and may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents Scroll Games in public spaces.
